GENERAL INFORMATION

What YourPath. actually is.

YourPath. is an AI-powered career exploration platform designed for students. Instead of matching you to a job title with a static quiz, it asks adaptive questions about your genuine interests, working style, motivation and the pressure around you — then explains the reasoning behind every pathway it suggests.

It is decision support, not a verdict. There is no “97% career match” here. Every pathway comes with its skills, education routes, trade-offs, alternatives, and one small real-world experiment you can run this month.

Quick links

General Information

YourPath. is an AI-powered career exploration platform for students. Adaptive questioning surfaces the interests, working styles and motivations that actually appear in your answers — then turns them into pathways you can test in the real world.

Every session asks 20 questions across ten dimensions, from genuine interests to external pressure. Selection weights describe how questions are chosen; they are never a personality score.

Is YourPath. safe to use?

Your privacy matters. Your account details and saved exploration progress are sent to the YourPath server so you can sign in and continue your journey. Some working data and preferences are also kept in this browser.

  • No third-party trackers, advertising pixels, or resale of student data.
  • No AI provider keys or student records are exposed in the frontend.
  • Export your data or clear saved progress in Settings → Privacy & data. You can also request account deletion there.
  • Pathways are explained as hypotheses to test, never as diagnoses or guarantees.

YourPath uses server-managed sessions and account-scoped access for saved progress. Deployments should use HTTPS and encrypted database storage, with documented retention and deletion rules.

FAQ

Common questions.

Short answers about how YourPath. works, what it stores, and what it will never claim to know.

Does YourPath. tell me which career to choose?

No. It shows several plausible directions with their reasoning, trade-offs and alternatives, plus one small experiment for each. The decision stays yours — and “I don’t know yet” is a normal, honest answer.

Where is my data stored?

Your account profile and saved exploration progress are stored by the YourPath server. This browser also keeps a working copy, preferences, and cookie choice. Use Settings to export your data, clear synced progress, or delete your account.

Can one questionnaire decide my future?

No, and any platform that claims otherwise is overselling. A 20-question session is one sample of evidence. Retaking it later and running real experiments gives better information.

How do I delete my account or my answers?

Log out, then clear this site’s data in your browser settings, or use Cookie settings in the footer. A production deployment would also provide an in-app deletion request flow.

Is this a replacement for a school counselor?

No. YourPath. is decision support. Teachers, counselors, parents and professionals should stay part of the conversation.

How do I report a problem?

Email support@yourpath.demo or post in the Discord community. Include the page you were on and what you expected to happen.

ABOUT YOURPATH.

For students, by purpose.

YourPath. is a guided career-exploration platform that helps students discover what genuinely fits them — and gives them a clear, honest path to get there.

What the website is for

YourPath. exists to replace guesswork with guided self-discovery. Instead of telling students what they "should" become, it helps them understand who they are and where they could go next. It is decision support — a structured space to think clearly about the future — not a replacement for teachers, counselors, parents or professional advice.

Our purpose

Career decisions are often made early, with too little information and too much pressure. YourPath. gives students an honest, low-risk way to explore many plausible directions before committing to one, so the choice is driven by genuine fit rather than by fear or the expectations of others.

What it does

  • Guided self-discovery — a 20-question questionnaire covers ten dimensions of interest, aptitude and preference, so the picture is built from many signals rather than a single answer.
  • Personalized pathways & roadmaps — the answers are turned into a shortlist of plausible pathways, each with contextual education, subject and skill guidance.
  • Visual insight — a radar map summarizes the patterns behind your answers, so you can see how your interests spread across the dimensions.
  • Saved pathways & notes — explore, shortlist and annotate the directions that interest you, and return to them whenever you need.
  • Experiments & real-world signals — small guided experiments let you test an interest in the real world and refine your picture over time.

How to read your results

Results describe patterns in your responses — not your worth, your ability or a fixed personality. They are a starting point for a conversation with yourself, your teachers and your family, and should always be verified against current, official sources before you rely on them.

PRIVACY POLICY

How YourPath. handles your information.

Your account details and saved exploration progress are sent to the YourPath server so your student workspace can sync. Your browser also keeps a working copy and preferences. This notice explains what is stored and which controls are available.

1. What we collect

  • Account details you type in: name, email, optional phone, country, grade, age and optional school.
  • Questionnaire answers, saved pathways, comparison choices and feedback you submit.
  • Preferences such as your cookie choice and login state.
  • We do not collect location data, contacts, camera or microphone access, or advertising identifiers.

2. Where it is stored

Account details and saved learning progress are stored on the YourPath server and synced with your browser. Your browser keeps a working copy, preferences and cookie choice. Feedback and anonymous usage evidence are stored locally. Use Settings to export your data, clear synced exploration progress, or permanently delete your account.

3. What we never do

  • No selling, renting or sharing of student data.
  • No third-party advertising or cross-site tracking pixels.
  • No AI provider keys, secrets or server credentials in the frontend.
  • No presenting a questionnaire result as a diagnosis or a guaranteed outcome.

4. Your rights and controls

  • Access — review your student details and saved exploration progress in the Dashboard and Settings.
  • Correction — edit your student details from Settings and save.
  • Deletion — clear exploration progress or permanently delete your account from Settings.
  • Withdrawal of consent — log out; no further data is collected until you sign in again.

5. Students and age

YourPath. is intended for students aged 13 and over. Where a country requires guardian consent, that requirement must be implemented before launch. Do not enter sensitive personal, medical or family information into this demo.

6. Before a real launch

  • Keep server sessions behind HTTPS and protect database backups and stored records with encryption.
  • Encrypt data in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and at rest; minimize what is collected in the first place.
  • Use role-based access control and audit logs for any staff access.
  • Implement age and guardian consent rules for every country served.
  • Document retention, correction and deletion procedures, and honour deletion requests.
  • Keep AI provider keys server-side and document the model provider's data-retention policy.
  • Use aggregated analytics rather than exposing identifiable student records.
  • Have student-facing guidance reviewed by qualified humans before publication.

7. Contact

Privacy questions or requests: support@yourpath.demo. See the for how the service may be used.
